DESIGN TIPS:
------------
1. Gate Drive:
   - Recommended VGS: 10-15V for full enhancement
   - Use gate resistor (10-100Ω) to control di/dt
   - Gate drive current: Ig = Qg × f (switching frequency)

2. Thermal Management:
   - Use heatsink for power dissipation > 2W
   - Thermal compound required for good heat transfer
   - Heatsink sizing: RθSA = (TJ(max) - TA) / PD - RθJC - RθCS

3. Switching Applications:
   - Lower RG = faster switching = higher EMI
   - Higher RG = slower switching = more switching loss
   - Typical RG: 10-47Ω for good compromise

4. Protection:
   - Use Zener diode (15-18V) from gate to source for overvoltage protection
   - Snubber network for inductive loads
   - Current sensing for overcurrent protection

TYPICAL APPLICATIONS:
---------------------
1. Buck Converter (Step-Down):
   Switching element in DC-DC buck converter
   Frequency: 50kHz-200kHz typical

2. Motor Driver:
   H-bridge configuration for bidirectional control
   PWM frequency: 20kHz-40kHz typical

3. Synchronous Rectifier:
   Replace Schottky diodes in high current, low voltage applications
   Reduces conduction losses
